Transistor as a Switch

Jan 29, 2023 5 min read

Aim

The aim of this study is to understand the working of a transistor as a switch and how it can be used to control the flow of current in a circuit.

Theory

A transistor can be used as a switch to control the flow of current in a circuit. The transistor operates in two modes: cut-off and saturation.

  • Cut-off Mode: The transistor acts as an open switch, and no current flows.
  • Saturation Mode: The transistor acts as a closed switch, and maximum current flows.

By adjusting the current applied to the base terminal of the transistor, we can control the flow of current in the circuit.

Procedure

  1. Connect the transistor to the breadboard as per the circuit diagram.
  2. Connect the power supply to the circuit.
  3. Apply a small current to the base terminal to observe the flow of current.
  4. Increase the base current and observe the effect.

Conclusion

The transistor is a versatile device that can be used as a switch in various electronic circuits. By understanding the basic principles of its operation, we can use it to design efficient and effective electronic circuits.
